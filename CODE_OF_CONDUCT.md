# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, the maintainer
and anyone participating in this project pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards others

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or
  advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, its
security advisories, its discussions, and any pull request — and in public
spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

This project does not accept public issues or external pull requests, so the
opportunity for conflict is small. Where it arises, the maintainer may remove,
edit or reject any contribution, comment or report that does not align with this
Code of Conduct, and may block accounts that behave abusively.

Abuse, harassment or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately
through [GitHub's abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) or
by opening a private security advisory on this repository. All reports will be
reviewed and treated confidentially.
