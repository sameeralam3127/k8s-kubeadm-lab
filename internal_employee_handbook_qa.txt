Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
IT Support
Q: How do I request a new laptop?
A: New laptop requests are handled through the IT Portal.

Log in to the IT Portal at https://itportal.yourcompany.com (internal network only).

Navigate to the "Equipment Request" section.

Select "New Hire Laptop" or "Replacement Laptop".

Fill out the form with your employee ID, department, and manager's name.

Your manager will receive an automated email for approval.

Once approved, the IT team will process your request. You will receive a notification email with an estimated delivery date within 2-3 business days.
Contact: If the portal is down, email it-helpdesk@yourcompany.com with your manager CC'd.

Q: My password is expired. How do I reset it?
A: You can reset your password yourself from the login screen.

On the Windows login screen, click "Reset password".

You will be prompted to answer your security questions.

After verifying your identity, you can set a new password. It must be at least 12 characters long and include a uppercase letter, a number, and a special symbol.
Contact: If you are locked out completely, you must call the IT Help Desk at extension 4357 (HELP).

Q: How do I get access to [Specific Software, e.g., Adobe Creative Cloud]?
A: Software access is based on departmental budgets.

First, get approval from your direct manager.

Once approved, your manager must submit a "Software Access Request" form via the IT Portal on your behalf.

IT will provision the software license and notify you once it is available for download in the company application portal.

Office & Facilities
Q: How do I book a meeting room?
A: All meeting rooms are booked through the company calendar system.

In Outlook or Google Calendar, create a new event.

Click "Add room" or "Find a time".

You will see a list of available rooms. Rooms with a screen icon are equipped with a TV and video conferencing.

Select the room you want and add it to your meeting invite.

The room is officially booked once you send the calendar invite. Your event will appear on the screen outside the meeting room.

Q: How do I get my access badge activated for the 4th floor?
A: Access to secure floors requires manager and security approval.

Your manager must email facilities@yourcompany.com and security@yourcompany.com with the request.

The email must include your full name, employee ID, and the reason for access.

Security will approve and notify Facilities. Your badge will be activated within 24 hours of approval. You can test it at any door reader.

Human Resources (HR)
Q: How do I update my tax withholding information?
A: You can update your W-4 information online.

Go to the Employee Self-Service (ESS) portal at https://ess.yourcompany.com.

Log in with your company credentials.

Navigate to the "Payroll" section and select "Tax Withholding".

Make your changes and submit them electronically. The changes will take effect on the next pay cycle.

Q: What is the policy for working from home?
A: Our company has a "Flexible Hybrid" policy.

Employees are expected to be in the office a minimum of 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are core days).

You must coordinate with your manager for your specific schedule.

All WFH requests must be logged in the HR system by your manager for tracking purposes. Please ensure you have a suitable home office setup and a reliable internet connection.

Q: Who do I contact to change my benefits?
A: For all benefits-related changes (except open enrollment), you must contact the HR benefits team directly. Please email hr-benefits@yourcompany.com to schedule a confidential call. Life events (marriage, birth of a child) qualify for a special enrollment period.

Onboarding
Q: What should I do on my first day?
A: Welcome! Your first day is about getting settled.

First Thing: Report to the front desk at 9:00 AM. They will have your temporary badge and will call your manager.

Meeting: You will have a meeting with HR to complete any remaining paperwork.

IT Setup: You will receive your new laptop and login credentials from the IT department. Please take this time to set it up.

Lunch: Your manager will take you to lunch with the team.

Afternoon: You will begin assigned onboarding modules in the company learning portal.

Q: Where can I find the onboarding checklist?
A: The comprehensive onboarding checklist for your first 30 days is located on the company intranet homepage. Navigate to the "Resources" section and click on "New Employee Onboarding". You must be on the internal network to access this.
